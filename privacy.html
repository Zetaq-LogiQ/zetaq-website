Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 1, 2026

Introduction

ZETAQ Private Limited ("ZETAQ," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform and AI tools. This policy is in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

ZETAQ Private Limited complies with Meta Platform Policies and WhatsApp Business Platform policies. We require all customers using WhatsApp features to comply with Meta's messaging rules and opt-in requirements.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Name, email address, and contact information
  • Account credentials (username and encrypted password)
  • Professional information (company name, job title, industry)
  • Payment and billing information (processed securely through third-party payment processors)
  • Communication preferences and correspondence with us
  • Usage Data
  • Device information (IP address, browser type, operating system)
  • Usage patterns and interactions with our AI tools
  • Log data and analytics information
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies
  • WhatsApp Business Data
  • When you connect your WhatsApp Business account to ZETAQ Private Limited, we may collect and process:
  • Phone numbers of end-users and business users
  • WhatsApp message content (inbound and outbound)
  • Message metadata (timestamps, delivery status)
  • Business profile information (business name, WhatsApp number)

This data is processed solely to provide WhatsApp automation, customer support, sales workflows, analytics, and AI-assisted responses as part of our Services.

AI-Generated Content

Input data provided to our AI tools and the resulting outputs may be temporarily stored for service improvement and quality assurance, in accordance with your consent.

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide, maintain, and improve our AI tools and platform
  • Account Management: To create and manage your user account
  • Communication: To send updates, technical notices, and support messages
  • Payment Processing: To process transactions and prevent fraud
  • Analytics: To understand usage patterns and improve our services
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable Indian laws and regulations
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address security incidents
  • WhatsApp Automation Purpose
  • We use WhatsApp message data and phone numbers to:
  • Send transactional and customer support messages
  • Provide AI-powered responses on behalf of businesses
  • Enable booking, order updates, and customer interactions
  • Maintain conversation history for customer service and compliance
  • WhatsApp Opt-In and Consent
  • We ensure that all WhatsApp communications comply with Meta's opt-in requirements:

End-users must provide explicit opt-in consent before receiving WhatsApp messages from businesses using our platform

Opt-in is collected by the business customer through their own channels (website forms, in-app prompts, or during service sign-up) and recorded in our system

End-users can opt out at any time by replying "STOP" to any WhatsApp message or by contacting the business directly

We maintain records of opt-in consent for audit and compliance purposes

Businesses using our platform are required to obtain and maintain valid opt-in consent from their end-users before initiating WhatsApp conversations

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data in the following circumstances:

Service Providers: With trusted third-party vendors who assist in operating our platform (cloud hosting, payment processing, analytics)

Legal Requirements: When required by Indian law, court order, or governmental authority

Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share specific information

WhatsApp Business Platform & BSP Sharing

To provide WhatsApp messaging functionality, we share limited necessary data with:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp Business Platform)
  • Authorized Business Solution Providers (BSPs) such as Twilio, Gupshup, 360dialog, or Meta Cloud API
  • Cloud infrastructure providers for secure message delivery and processing

We do not sell WhatsApp user data. Data is shared only to enable message delivery, compliance, monitoring, and platform functionality.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data:

  • Data encryption in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms
  • Secure data centers with physical and digital safeguards
  • Employee training on data protection and privacy

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but will notify you promptly of any data breach as required under Section 43A of the IT Act, 2000.

WhatsApp Data Security

For WhatsApp-related data, we implement additional safeguards:

  • All WhatsApp API communications use TLS 1.2+ encryption
  • WhatsApp message content is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption
  • Access to WhatsApp data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis

API tokens and WhatsApp Business Account credentials are stored in encrypted vaults and never exposed in logs or client-side code

We do not store WhatsApp end-user passwords or payment information

Regular security assessments are conducted on our WhatsApp integration infrastructure

Your Rights Under Indian Law

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: File complaints regarding privacy violations

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at the contact details provided below.

WhatsApp Data Deletion Requests

In addition to the rights above, end-users and business customers may request deletion of WhatsApp-related data:

End-users: Can request deletion of their WhatsApp conversation data by contacting the business they interacted with, or by emailing us directly at support@zetaq.in

Business customers: Can request deletion of all WhatsApp data associated with their account through their dashboard settings or by contacting support@zetaq.in

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days of receipt

Upon processing, all WhatsApp message content, phone numbers, and metadata associated with the request are permanently removed from our systems

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, or as required by Indian law. Account data is retained for the duration of your active subscription and up to 3 years thereafter for legal and accounting purposes. AI interaction logs are anonymized after 90 days unless you request earlier deletion.

WhatsApp Data Retention

WhatsApp message content and metadata are retained for a maximum of 90 days from the date of the message to fulfill service delivery, customer support, and compliance obligations. After this period, message content is permanently deleted or anonymized. Specifically:

  • WhatsApp message content (text, media): retained for up to 90 days, then permanently deleted
  • Phone numbers and contact data: retained for the duration of the business customer's active subscription

Message metadata (delivery status, timestamps): retained for up to 90 days for analytics, then aggregated and anonymized

WhatsApp Business profile information: retained for the duration of the active integration

Upon disconnection of a WhatsApp Business account or termination of services, all associated WhatsApp data is deleted within 30 days, unless retention is required by law.

International Data Transfers

Your data is primarily stored on servers located in India. If data is transferred outside India, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses and adequate data protection measures in compliance with Indian regulations.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain session state and user preferences
  • Analyze platform usage and performance
  • Personalize your experience
  • Enhance security and prevent fraud

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect platform functionality.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will delete such information from our systems.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of our services after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
